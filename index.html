Growth Stalls for Four Reasons

技术不差、服务不差，问题从来不是一件事。对号入座，看你缺哪一件。

Good skills, good service — the problem is never just one thing. Find yours below.

😶

① 别人不知道你

① Nobody knows you

Google 搜"附近美甲"，出来的全是竞争对手。你没有网站，没有 Google 地图标注，没人知道你在哪。

Search "nail salon nearby" and only competitors show up. No website, no Google Maps listing — you're invisible to new customers.

🫥

② 知道了也记不住你

② They can't remember you

客人挺满意，一个月后想再来，只记得"那家中餐"，想不起店名。Google 上 23 条评价，最新一条两年前，两条差评没人回——他退出去点了隔壁。

A happy customer wants to come back a month later, but only remembers "that Chinese place." On Google: 23 reviews, the latest two years old, two bad ones unanswered — so they clicked the shop next door instead.

🔄

③ 来过一次就不再来

③ They never come back

客人做完就走，下次来不来靠缘分。没有积分、没有会员、没有核销记录——你连谁来过第二次都不知道，做活动只能广撒网打折。

Customers visit once and disappear. No points, no membership, no redemption records — you don't even know who came twice, so every promotion is a blanket discount.

😮‍💨

④ 一忙起来全靠人扛

④ Everything runs on manual labour

接单靠电话、排班靠微信、对账靠 Excel、库存靠脑子。生意一大就得多雇人，雇了人还是乱，利润全被人工吃掉。

Orders by phone, rosters over WeChat, reconciliation in Excel, stock in your head. Growth means hiring more people — and it's still chaos, with wages eating the margin.
