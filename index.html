生意做不大，通常是这四件事

Growth Stalls for Four Reasons

技术不差、服务不差，问题从来不是一件事。对号入座，看你缺哪一件。

Good skills, good service — the problem is never just one thing. Find yours below.

😶

① 别人不知道你

① Nobody knows you

Google 搜"附近美甲"，出来的全是竞争对手。你没有网站，没有 Google 地图标注，没人知道你在哪。

Search "nail salon nearby" and only competitors show up. No website, no Google Maps listing — you're invisible to new customers.

🫥

② 知道了也记不住你

② They can't remember you

客人挺满意，一个月后想再来，只记得"那家中餐"，想不起店名。Google 上 23 条评价，最新一条两年前，两条差评没人回——他退出去点了隔壁。

A happy customer wants to come back a month later, but only remembers "that Chinese place." On Google: 23 reviews, the latest two years old, two bad ones unanswered — so they clicked the shop next door instead.

🔄

③ 来过一次就不再来

③ They never come back

客人做完就走，下次来不来靠缘分。没有积分、没有会员、没有优惠券记录——你连谁来过第二次都不知道，做活动只能广撒网打折。

Customers visit once and disappear. No points, no membership, no coupon records — you don't even know who came twice, so every promotion is a blanket discount.

😮‍💨

④ 一忙起来全靠人扛

④ Everything runs on manual labour

接单靠电话、排班靠微信、对账靠 Excel、库存靠脑子。生意一大就得多雇人，雇了人还是乱，利润全被人工吃掉。

Orders by phone, rosters over WeChat, reconciliation in Excel, stock in your head. Growth means hiring more people — and it's still chaos, with wages eating the margin.

② 让人记住你 Be Remembered

网站有了，还得让人记得住

The Site Is Live. Now Make Them Remember.

让人记住你不是一件事，是四件事按顺序做——先在地图上被看见，再在评价里被信任，然后每月反复出现在他手机上，最后让他能一句话跟朋友说清你是谁。每月做多少条，合同里写死

Being remembered isn't one thing — it's four, in order: get seen on the map, get trusted in the reviews, turn up in their feed every month, and finally give them one sentence they can repeat to a friend. How many of each per month is written into the contract

📍

先在地图上被看见

Get seen on the map first

Google 商户资料的营业时间、地址、照片、简介全部校正到位，每月 4 条商户动态让它一直是「活的」。客人搜「附近＋你这行」，第一眼看到的是你的资料页，不是隔壁的。

Opening hours, address, photos and description on your Google Business Profile all corrected, plus 4 posts a month so it never looks abandoned. When someone searches "near me + your trade", your profile is what they see first — not the shop next door.

⭐

再在评价里被信任

Then be trusted in the reviews

每条新评价 48 小时内中英双语回复，差评优先处理。客人真正在看的其实是两个数字：一共多少条、最新一条是什么时候。最新一条停在两年前，比一条都没有更劝退。

Every new review answered within 48 hours in both languages, negative ones first. Customers really only read two numbers: how many reviews, and how recent. A latest review from two years ago puts people off more than having none.

📱

让他一个月刷到你好几次

Keep turning up in their feed

TikTok / Instagram / Facebook 每月固定条数成稿，小红书触达本地华人客群。三个渠道共用一套视觉——人一般要刷到第三次才会记住你是谁，所以频次比单条做得多精致更要紧。

A fixed number of TikTok / Instagram / Facebook posts every month, plus Xiaohongshu for the local Chinese community. One visual system across all three — people tend to remember you around the third time they see you, so frequency matters more than polish on any single post.

🗣️

最后让口碑能被复述

Finally, make it repeatable

一句话主张、招牌项命名、视觉锤，配上桌卡、门贴、包装贴纸。客人跟朋友讲起你的时候，得能用一句话说清你是谁——他说不出来，口碑就传不下去。

A one-line claim, a named signature item, a visual hook, backed by table cards, door decals and packaging stickers. When a customer tells a friend about you, they need one sentence that lands — if they can't say it, word of mouth stops there.
