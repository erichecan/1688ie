Your business is great.
But nobody knows it.
That's the real problem.

在爱尔兰做小生意，技术不差、服务不差，但生意就是难做大。 根源往往是同一件事：没有系统——没有稳定的获客渠道，没有留住老客户的方法，没有让生意自己跑起来的工具。

Running a small business in Ireland — good skills, good service, but growth stalls. The root cause is almost always the same: no system — no reliable way to attract customers, no way to retain regulars, no tools to make the business run itself.

😶

搜不到你

You're invisible online

Google 搜"附近美甲"，出来的全是竞争对手。你没有网站，没有 Google 地图标注，没人知道你在哪。

Search "nail salon nearby" and only competitors show up. No website, no Google Maps listing — you're invisible to new customers.

📞

全靠口口相传

100% word of mouth

新客人全靠老客介绍，淡季一来生意就断。没有主动获客的方式，生意每年都在靠运气。

Every new customer comes from a referral. In slow seasons, business dries up completely. Growth depends entirely on luck.

🔄

老客来了就走，留不住

One-time visitors, no repeat business

客人做完就走，下次来不来靠缘分。没有积分、没有会员、没有优惠券，没有任何理由让他们回来。

Customers visit once and disappear. No loyalty points, no membership, no coupons — nothing to bring them back.

🌐

接不了线上订单

Can't take orders online

客人想网上下单、提前预约、买礼品卡，你给不了。每笔生意都要电话或者当面谈，错失大量收入。

Customers want to order online, book ahead, buy gift cards. You can't offer that — every sale requires a phone call or walk-in.
