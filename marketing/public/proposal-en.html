02Why all four move together

Say a thousand people come across you this month: 180 click, 36 show up, 7 come back. Double the ad spend and those seven become fourteen. Give people a reason to stay and a reason to return, and the same thousand are worth three or four times as much. Stage one is the only one you have to keep paying for; the other three are fixed once and pay out indefinitely. Fix the back end first, then turn up the front — reverse that order and you are burning money.

The last two stages are rare in this market because they need software rather than posts: review pipelines, loyalty points, spend dashboards. The loyalty system comes from the 1688.ie Essential Wangpu plan (built in-house, not resold SaaS) — a one-off build on your own website, data yours, nobody taking a cut. Day-to-day operation — scan-to-join, points redemption, member campaigns — is what the Full cycle plan does. Most marketing agencies can't deliver this stage at all.

  7. Publishing. Instagram and Facebook posts are published by you or a team member you designate. We do not take custody of your accounts and do not publish on your behalf.
  8. Intellectual property. On payment, all copy, images and materials delivered are yours to use without restriction.
  9. Member data. The loyalty system deploys on your domain and the database is yours, exportable at any time. On termination we hand over the data and back-office access in full.

  • Account custody. We do not take ownership of your accounts and do not hold your credentials.
  • Paid advertising. Google Ads, Meta Ads and TikTok Ads spend and management are not included.

  • Loyalty system third-party costs. SMS and email delivery, and payment processing fees, are billed at cost; the Essential Wangpu plan's build and hosting fee is quoted separately above under "Full cycle prerequisite."
  • Profile ownership recovery. If your Google profile is held by a former vendor, we can run the claim from €350; the outcome is Google's decision.
