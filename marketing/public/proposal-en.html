02Why all four move together

Say a thousand people come across you this month: 180 click, 36 show up, 7 come back. Double the ad spend and those seven become fourteen. Give people a reason to stay and a reason to return, and the same thousand are worth three or four times as much. Stage one is the only one you have to keep paying for; the other three are fixed once and pay out indefinitely. Fix the back end first, then turn up the front — reverse that order and you are burning money.

The last two stages are rare in this market because they need software rather than posts: review pipelines, loyalty points, spend dashboards. We build the loyalty system ourselves. It deploys on your domain and the database is yours.

07Terms

  1. Validity. This proposal is valid for 30 days from the date issued.
  2. Payment. Billed monthly in advance by e-Transfer or cheque. Prices exclude HST (13% in Ontario).
  3. No contract. No minimum term. Cancel with 15 days' written notice; the current month is not prorated.
  4. Month-one guarantee. If the first month's delivery disappoints you, remaining prepaid months are refunded in full.
  5. Account security. We never request or store your login credentials. Access is granted through each platform's official consent flow and can be revoked by you at any time.
  6. Filming. Clips are filmed by you from our shot list unless a separate shoot day is quoted. We edit, caption and write every hook.
  7. Publishing. Where an official scheduling API is available and approved, we publish on your behalf. Otherwise finished files are delivered for you to post.
  8. Intellectual property. On payment, all copy, images and video delivered are yours to use without restriction.
  9. Member data. The loyalty system deploys on your domain and the database is yours, exportable at any time. On termination we hand over the data and back-office access in full.
  10. Source material. Premises, product and staff footage is supplied by you, and you confirm you hold the rights and releases to use it.

08Not included

  • Guaranteed views, followers or ranking. We commit to monthly output volume and quality. Reach and ranking sit with the platforms; we do not promise them and do not bill against them.
  • Review removal. Removal is the platform's decision. We write replies and can file appeals ($299 each), without guaranteeing the outcome.
  • Account custody. We do not take ownership of your accounts and do not hold your credentials.
  • Paid advertising. Google Ads, Meta Ads and TikTok Ads spend and management are not included.
  • Professional shoots. On-location filming, crew and talent are quoted separately; this proposal covers editing, design and copy.
  • Loyalty hardware and third-party costs. Scanners, payment processing fees, SMS and email delivery, and hosting are billed at cost.
  • Profile ownership recovery. If your Google profile is held by a former vendor, we can run the claim from $399; the outcome is Google's decision.
