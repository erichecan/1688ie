常见问题

Frequently Asked

销售前最常被问到的 6 个问题。

The 6 most common questions before signing.

价格是含 VAT 还是 ex VAT？ Are prices VAT-inclusive or ex VAT?

所有展示价格均为 ex VAT（税前）。爱尔兰 VAT 标准税率 23%，VAT 注册企业可全额抵扣进项税； 非 VAT 注册客户实际支付需加 23% VAT。正式 Proposal 中会明列 Subtotal / VAT @ 23% / Total 三行。

All listed prices are ex VAT. Ireland's standard VAT is 23% and VAT-registered businesses can fully reclaim it. Non-VAT customers pay an extra 23%. Every Proposal lists Subtotal / VAT @ 23% / Total separately.

我可以先选 STARTER，以后再升级吗？ Can I start with STARTER and upgrade later?

可以。所有档位之间支持平滑升级，升级时只需补差价 + 调整月费。如果未来确定要做电商或连锁管理， 建议直接选 ESSENTIAL / GROWTH，一步到位比两次升级省约 €500-1,000。

Yes. Tiers support seamless upgrades — pay the price delta plus the new monthly fee. If you already plan e-commerce or chain ops, starting at ESSENTIAL / GROWTH typically saves €500-1,000 vs. two staged upgrades.

LEO Trading Online Voucher 怎么申请？ How do I apply for the LEO Trading Online Voucher?

符合条件的微型企业（<10 员工、年营业额 <€2M、注册满 6 个月且在爱尔兰运营）可申请最高 €2,500 补贴， 采用 50/50 匹配模式（客户先出 50%，LEO 批准后返还 50%）。1688.ie 提供 €299 申请辅助服务， 包括填表、附文档、与本地 LEO 对接。申请周期 4-8 周。

Eligible micro-enterprises (<10 staff, <€2M turnover, 6+ months registered, trading in Ireland) can claim up to €2,500 via 50/50 match (you pay 50% upfront, LEO refunds 50% on approval). 1688.ie offers €299 application support covering forms, documents and LEO liaison. Turnaround 4-8 weeks.

合同期多久？可以提前终止吗？ What is the contract term? Can I exit early?

标准月费合约为 12 个月最低承诺（ENTERPRISE 及以上为 24 个月）。合约期内如需提前终止， 需支付剩余月数 30% 作为违约金。续约时享 10% 折扣或免费升档 3 个月试用。

Standard SaaS contracts are a 12-month minimum commitment (24 months for ENTERPRISE+). Early termination requires 30% of remaining monthly fees. Renewal: 10% discount or free 3-month upgrade trial.

月费具体包含什么？ What does the monthly fee include?

托管 + SSL + 日备份 + 安全补丁 + 基础维护（每月固定小改动时数）+ 中文客服 + 数据导出 + 季度业务回顾（GROWTH 及以上）+ 月度 2 小时 CSM 咨询（ENTERPRISE）。 超出小改动时数部分按 €60/小时额外计算，账单按月分开明列，绝无隐藏费用。

Hosting + SSL + daily backups + security patches + maintenance hours + Chinese-language support + data export + quarterly review (GROWTH+) + monthly 2h CSM session (ENTERPRISE). Overage hours billed at €60/hr, itemised monthly. No hidden fees.

可以只买建站不买月费吗？ Can I buy build-only without monthly fees?

不建议。月费包含托管、SSL、GDPR 合规更新、安全补丁——没有月费的网站在 3-6 个月内就可能因为证书过期、 插件未更新而被搜索引擎降权或被黑。如果您现有技术团队能自行维护，可选 BESPOKE 源码交付（+€30,000 起）， 一次性买断后自行托管。

Not recommended. The monthly fee covers hosting, SSL, compliance updates and security patches — sites without it can lose ranking or be hacked within 3-6 months due to expired certs or unpatched plugins. If you have an in-house team, choose BESPOKE source delivery (from +€30,000) for self-hosting.
